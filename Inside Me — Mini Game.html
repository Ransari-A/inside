Case 07 · Patient briefing

“I can’t catch my breath.”

A 54-year-old patient had knee surgery three days ago and has spent most of that time in bed. Today they suddenly became short of breath, with chest pain when breathing in.

Your mission: you'll be injected into a vein in the patient's left leg. Follow the flow of blood to the lungs, avoid immune cells, collect data fragments, and look out for evidence along the way.

What you learned
  • Long periods without moving after surgery can cause a clot in a leg vein (DVT).
  • Venous blood flows leg → vena cava → right atrium → right ventricle → pulmonary artery → lungs.
  • A clot that lodges in the lung's arteries is a pulmonary embolism: airways are clear, but blood flow stops, so oxygen falls.
